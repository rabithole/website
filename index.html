Who Am I?

Systems Thinking

Decades of automotive diagnostics, fabrication and repair is all it took for me to discover how my mind works. Really it was a few work colleagues become friends, that have shown me, I am a systems thinker.

With hands-on experience of every aspect of vehicle engineering, I continue to expand my knowledge base and experience by pursuing a degree in Mechanical Engineering. The automobiles our world survives by are the most complicated and complete works of engineering achievements that the human race has seen. Working on vehicles afforded me insight into the scientific disciplines that are the building blocks of our existence. This granted me the opportunity to understand the world as a grouping of systems to which details are there to see as I choose. Science teaches us explanations about how our world works. With this knowledge, we can create amazing things when we choose to work cooperatively, opposed to competitively.

Another aspect of this website, is it affords me the opportunity to share more thoughts about the world that is all around us. Few things I believe absolutely and am always open to discussion with others of different perspectives, as this affords me insight I could not see from my own, limited perspective. This leads to my philosophy of life that I choose to live. Find your way to my blog to read and hopefully discuss our world.

Let’s discover the world together, one perspective at a time!
